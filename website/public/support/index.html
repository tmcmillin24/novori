A few useful answers

How do I reset my password?

Tap Forgot password on Novori's sign-in screen. Enter your account email and open the newest reset email on your phone. If a link has expired, request a fresh one.

How do I open a shared book or post?

Open the shared link on a phone with Novori installed, then tap the button to open it in the app. Book stacks work the same way.

Can I share a suggestion?

Absolutely. Email us with what you would like to see and how it would help your reading life.
